Event Study Visualization Notes

This document explains the six figures generated from the daily event-study data around the SVB collapse event on 2023-03-10.

Figure 1: Daily Returns by Group

This figure shows the raw daily returns of the four groups: Treatment Big, Treatment Small, Control Big, and Control Small.

What it means:
- It compares how each group moved on each trading day in the event window.
- The vertical dashed line marks the event date.
- The title of each panel shows the average daily return for that group in the window.

What it shows:
- Both treatment groups experienced larger negative returns than the control groups.
- The Treatment Small group was hit the hardest.
- The post-event days show stronger pressure for the high-exposure banks.

Conclusion:
- Banks with higher exposure to the banking shock reacted more negatively than the control firms.
- Smaller exposed banks were more sensitive than larger exposed banks.

Figure 2: Market-Adjusted Returns by Group

This figure shows daily returns minus the market return of SPY for each group.

What it means:
- It removes broad market movement and focuses on abnormal performance relative to the market.
- A more negative value means the stock underperformed the market more strongly.

What it shows:
- Treatment Small has the most negative average market-adjusted return.
- Treatment Big is also clearly negative.
- Control groups are much closer to zero.

Conclusion:
- The event caused idiosyncratic negative abnormal returns in the exposed banks, not just a general market decline.
- This supports the idea that the SVB collapse had a direct impact on vulnerable regional banks.

Figure 3: Group Comparison

This bar chart compares the average daily return and average market-adjusted return across the four groups.

What it means:
- It provides one summary number for each group.
- It is useful for a high-level comparison of event severity.

What it shows:
- Treatment Small has the worst average return and worst market-adjusted return.
- Treatment Big is the second most affected.
- Control Big performs best, followed by Control Small.

Conclusion:
- There is a clear exposure effect.
- The more exposed the group is to the banking shock, the more negative the return.

Figure 4: Heatmap of Market-Adjusted Returns by Group and Date

This heatmap shows the average market-adjusted return for each group on each event-window trading date.

What it means:
- Darker red indicates stronger underperformance relative to SPY.
- Green indicates outperformance.
- It helps identify when the biggest shock happened.

What it shows:
- The most severe negative values appear around 2023-03-13, the first trading day after the weekend following the SVB collapse.
- Treatment Small has the strongest negative spike.
- Control groups remain much less negative.

Conclusion:
- The market reaction intensified after the event and after the weekend.
- The shock propagated most strongly to the vulnerable regional banks.

Figure 5: Individual Stock Returns Heatmap

This heatmap shows market-adjusted returns for each individual stock, separated into treatment and control panels.

What it means:
- It shows which specific stocks drove the group-level result.
- It allows you to see within-group heterogeneity.

What it shows:
- Treatment stocks such as WAL, KEY, ZION, and CMA show particularly large negative abnormal returns.
- Control stocks are more stable and usually less negative.
- Some control financial names can still be affected, but the magnitude is smaller.

Conclusion:
- The impact is not uniform even within the treatment group.
- The smallest and more exposed regional banks experience the strongest losses.

Figure 6: Cumulative Market-Adjusted Returns

This line chart shows cumulative market-adjusted returns across the event window.

What it means:
- It tracks the accumulated abnormal performance over time.
- If the line keeps falling, the group keeps underperforming the market.

What it shows:
- Treatment Small has the steepest cumulative decline.
- Treatment Big also declines strongly.
- Control Big and Control Small decline less.

Conclusion:
- The event produced persistent negative abnormal performance, not just a one-day reaction.
- The treatment groups suffered a larger cumulative loss than the control groups.

Overall Conclusion

The six figures together show a consistent pattern:
- The SVB collapse had a clear negative effect on exposed regional banks.
- Smaller exposed banks were hit the hardest.
- Large diversified financial firms were less affected.
- The abnormal returns are negative even after controlling for the market, which supports the event-study interpretation.

In short, the evidence suggests that the SVB collapse disproportionately hurt banks with higher exposure and weaker perceived resilience, especially the small regional banks in the treatment group.

事件研究图表说明（中文翻译）

本文说明了基于 2023-03-10 硅谷银行倒闭事件生成的 6 张事件研究图。

图 1：各组日收益率

这张图展示了四个组别的原始日收益率：Treatment Big、Treatment Small、Control Big 和 Control Small。

它的含义：
- 用来比较事件窗口内各组在每个交易日的涨跌情况。
- 红色虚线表示事件发生日。
- 每个子图标题中的平均值表示该组在窗口期内的平均日收益率。

它显示的结果：
- 两个 treatment 组的负收益都明显大于 control 组。
- Treatment Small 受到的冲击最严重。
- 事件后的交易日，高暴露银行承受了更强的压力。

结论：
- 对银行冲击暴露越高，反应越负面。
- 小型高暴露银行比大型高暴露银行更敏感。

图 2：市场调整后的日收益率

这张图展示的是每个组别的日收益率减去 SPY 市场收益率后的结果。

它的含义：
- 它剔除了整体市场波动，强调股票相对于市场的异常表现。
- 数值越负，表示相对于市场的表现越差。

它显示的结果：
- Treatment Small 的平均市场调整收益率最差。
- Treatment Big 也明显为负。
- 两个 control 组更接近 0。

结论：
- 这次事件带来了显著的个股异常负收益，而不只是整体市场下跌。
- 说明 SVB 倒闭对脆弱的区域性银行有直接冲击。

图 3：组别对比

这张柱状图比较了四个组别的平均日收益率和平均市场调整收益率。

它的含义：
- 用单个统计值概括每个组的表现。
- 适合做事件冲击强弱的高层比较。

它显示的结果：
- Treatment Small 的平均收益率和市场调整收益率都最差。
- Treatment Big 排在第二。
- Control Big 表现最好，其次是 Control Small。

结论：
- 存在非常清晰的暴露度效应。
- 对银行冲击暴露越高，收益率越负。

图 4：按组别和日期划分的市场调整收益率热力图

这张热力图展示了每个组在事件窗口内每个交易日的平均市场调整收益率。

它的含义：
- 颜色越红，表示相对 SPY 的表现越差。
- 绿色表示相对市场跑赢。
- 它有助于判断冲击主要发生在哪些日期。

它显示的结果：
- 最严重的负值出现在 2023-03-13，也就是 SVB 倒闭后周末过后的第一个交易日。
- Treatment Small 的负冲击最强。
- control 组整体明显更稳定。

结论：
- 市场反应在事件后以及周末之后进一步加剧。
- 冲击主要传导到了脆弱的区域性银行。

图 5：个股市场调整收益率热力图

这张热力图展示了每只股票的市场调整收益率，并分别放在 treatment 和 control 两个面板中。

它的含义：
- 用来识别到底是哪些股票驱动了组别层面的结果。
- 可以观察组内异质性。

它显示的结果：
- WAL、KEY、ZION、CMA 等 treatment 股票出现了特别大的负异常收益。
- control 股票整体更稳定，通常负幅度较小。
- 某些 control 金融公司也会受到影响，但幅度明显更小。

结论：
- treatment 组内部并不是完全一致的。
- 规模更小、暴露更高的区域性银行承受了最强损失。

图 6：累积市场调整收益率

这张折线图展示了事件窗口内各组的累积市场调整收益率。

它的含义：
- 它追踪的是一段时间内累计起来的异常表现。
- 如果曲线持续下跌，说明该组持续跑输市场。

它显示的结果：
- Treatment Small 的累积下降最陡。
- Treatment Big 也明显下滑。
- Control Big 和 Control Small 的跌幅较小。

结论：
- 这次事件带来的是持续性的负异常表现，而不是单日反应。
- treatment 组的累计损失显著大于 control 组。

总体结论

这 6 张图整体上呈现出非常一致的模式：
- SVB 倒闭对高暴露的区域性银行造成了明显负面影响。
- 小型高暴露银行受到的冲击最严重。
- 大型、多元化金融机构受到的影响较小。
- 即使控制了市场因素，异常收益率仍然为负，这支持了事件研究的解释。

简而言之，证据表明，SVB 倒闭对暴露程度更高、抗冲击能力更弱的银行造成了不成比例的伤害，尤其是 treatment 组中的小型区域性银行。
